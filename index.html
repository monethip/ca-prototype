Activation is an API call with a payment reference — not a manual queue.

Signet calls the CA with the confirmed payment reference. The CA updates entitlements and provisions the certificate for the selected package and duration. Success or failure returns to the portal. The subscriber is notified by email or in-app message.

Sequential process

Six steps. Four systems. One outcome: a paid, activated certificate.

1

Authentication and session

Subscriber authenticates on the Signet login page. The portal issues the session.

2

Access the payment portal

After login, the user stays in Signet to configure the purchase.

3

Purchase configuration

Package and duration selected. Portal calculates the invoice and creates a PSP transaction.

4

Transaction execution

User pays via QR or link. Gateway confirms with webhook or callback.

5

Invoice generation

Portal generates the invoice. User views, downloads, or exports it.

6

Certificate activation

Portal sends the payment reference. CA provisions the certificate and notifies the user.
